About Us

Women and Children Welfare Society (WCWS) is a non-governmental organization dedicated to improving the lives of women and children in Nepal through education, empowerment, and healthcare initiatives.

Since its founding, WCWS has been committed to uplifting rural communities by providing educational opportunities, women empowerment programs, healthcare assistance, and emergency aid during crises. Our vision is to create a just and equitable society where women and children can thrive with dignity and independence.
